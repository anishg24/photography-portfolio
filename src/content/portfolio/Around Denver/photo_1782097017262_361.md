Got a photo of this well-behaved *[Dark-Billed Magpie](https://www.audubon.org/field-guide/bird/dark-billed-magpie)* resting on a fence post.

What's really good about this photo that I didn't notice earlier was the bokeh pattern behind the bird. Because I use a normal non-anomorphic lens, the bokeh pattern is circular with the bird at the center.
