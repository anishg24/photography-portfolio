> "Today was good. Today was fun. Tomorrow is another one."
> 
> — Dr. Seuss, *One Fish Two Fish Red Fish Blue Fish*

Graduation is a milestone that I never doubted would come, but a milestone that I never thought to look past. For the longest time, graduation was the end goal, that for all 22 years of my life this was the finish line that I had to cross.

But I only have recently realized that I didn't see what was waiting for me on the other side of this milestone. A new journey that I am incredibly excited to embark on. One with new challenges, new people, and new experiences.

I'd be a fool to not have fun.
