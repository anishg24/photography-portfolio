Dr. Suess was one of my favorite early authors growing up. Its interesting how easy it is to read the stories he did as a kid and extract some meaning, but as an adult you can read into the lines and find much deeper meanings.

It's almost like a gift to be able to understand something so complex and deep, issues that are hard to grasp without the life experience to go through them. Dr. Seuss certainly did—he had his ups and downs, and people know him as both famous and infamous. It's an undervalued skill to take a concept so complex and present it in such an elegant manner that even 4 year olds can read and understand it. 

I went to an art gallery recently in San Diego that had some duplicates of his original concept art and sketches, like the ones for *Green Eggs and Ham* and *The Cat in the Hat* priced around $800. It's crazy that from such a simple drawing, you can get an insight into how he was doing; none more exaggerated than in his midnight paintings. 

I never really understood art, so I guess Dr. Seuss also gave me my introduction to how to read it. This one's for you, Doctor.
